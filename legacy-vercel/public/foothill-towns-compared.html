Calaveras County · town comparison

Arnold, Murphys, Angels Camp or Copperopolis — which foothill town fits you?

Four Calaveras County towns, four different lives. The right one comes down to elevation, commute, water and what you want out of a Saturday.

The four towns
Arnold, Murphys, Angels Camp and Copperopolis are the four best-known towns of the Calaveras County foothills. Arnold is the high, forested cabin town; Murphys is walkable wine country; Angels Camp is the county's only incorporated city and shortest-commute value play; Copperopolis is the low, warm lake-and-golf town. All four are in Calaveras County and share Bret Harte Union High School.

How to choose

How to choose.

If you want snow and a cabin: Arnold.

Arnold sits highest, at about 3,999 feet on the Highway 4 corridor, with real winter snow and quick access to Bear Valley skiing and Calaveras Big Trees State Park. Arnold is the group's genuine second-home and cabin market — and the one where wildfire insurance matters most.

If you want walkable wine-country town life: Murphys.

Murphys packs more than two dozen tasting rooms into a four-block Main Street at 2,171 feet, with an 1856 hotel and a creekside park. Murphys draws Bay Area and Sacramento buyers who want to trade a commute for a porch without giving up somewhere to walk to.

If you want value and the shortest commute: Angels Camp.

Angels Camp is the only incorporated city in Calaveras County, the lowest-priced of these town cores, and the closest to the Central Valley — about an hour to Stockton. Angels Camp is also home to Bret Harte High School and the Jumping Frog Jubilee.

If you want the lake and the golf: Copperopolis.

Copperopolis is the warmest and lowest, at 997 feet on Lake Tulloch — one of the few California reservoirs allowing private shoreline homes — anchored by the Copper Valley and Saddle Creek golf communities.

Common questions

Common questions.

Which Calaveras foothill town has the shortest Central Valley commute?

Copperopolis has the shortest Central Valley commute of the four, at about 49 minutes to Stockton, followed by Angels Camp at roughly one hour. Arnold and Murphys sit higher on Highway 4 and are farther out.

Which foothill town gets snow — Arnold or Murphys?

Arnold, at about 3,999 feet, is the true snow town of the group and functions as a cabin and ski second-home market near Bear Valley. Murphys, at 2,171 feet, sees occasional snow but is milder. Angels Camp (1,378 ft) and Copperopolis (997 ft) are the warmest and lowest.

Which town is best for lakefront property?

Copperopolis is the lakefront choice, built around Lake Tulloch — one of the few California reservoirs that permits private shoreline homes — plus the Copper Valley and Saddle Creek golf communities.

Which town is the wine-country pick?

Murphys is the wine town of Calaveras County, with more than two dozen tasting rooms along a walkable four-block Main Street. For Amador County vineyard estates, look one county north to Sutter Creek and the Shenandoah Valley.

Are all four towns in the same county and school system?

All four — Arnold, Murphys, Angels Camp and Copperopolis — are in Calaveras County and feed Bret Harte Union High School. Elementary districts differ: Vallecito Union serves Arnold and Murphys, while Mark Twain Union serves Angels Camp and Copperopolis.
