Choosing the headline font.

Fraunces (what the site uses now) is characterful and luxury, but its soft, high-contrast letterforms can get hard to read — especially in the light italic and at smaller heading sizes. Here are four alternatives that keep the boutique-luxury feel while reading more cleanly. Each block below shows the same real elements from your site.

Only the headline serif changes. Body copy, labels, and the mono eyebrows stay exactly as they are. Scan for readability — the wordmark, the big italic greeting, the section title, and the mid-size line. When one feels right, tell me its name and I'll switch the whole site to it.
