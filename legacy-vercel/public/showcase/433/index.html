The residence

Nineteenth-century craft, twenty-first-century comfort

Ten-foot ceilings, intricate original woodwork and period hardware, carried through a luxury remodel that never trades away authenticity. A wrap-around porch made for long evenings. Four bedrooms, three baths, 2,854 square feet.

The residence is delivered vacant. It was leased at $3,000 per month NNN until a prior escrow required vacant possession; that buyer withdrew five days before closing. The house can be re-leased on the same terms — and given today's market, likely somewhat higher. Move in, or place a tenant.
