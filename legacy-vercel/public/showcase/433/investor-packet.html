Overview

What the property actually is, in one pass

Historic mixed-use estate, one working tenant, one vacant house, commercial zoning that's stayed consistent for at least 15 years of MLS history.

The parcel

A single legal parcel — Adjusted Parcel B per the 2016 Record of Survey lot line adjustment (Book 24, Page 18), a portion of Lot 26, Block 7, Murphys Townsite. Fronts Highway 4, the only road into Murphys from the west. Zoned C‑1, General Plan designation Commercial, confirmed independently across the county assessor's GIS parcel record, the November 2025 preliminary title report, and 15 years of MLS listing history back to 2010.

The buildings

A restored 4‑bed/3‑bath farmhouse dated 1902 by the assessor (other listings over the years have dated it as early as 1850), 2,854 SF; a detached ~900 SF commercial building currently leased to a wellness tenant; a partially converted garage/outbuilding with a roughed-in bath; a shed and a shade structure noted on the 2025 parcel survey.

Rear aerial, farmhouse and garage/outbuilding · Nov 2025 drone photography

Listing status today

Active at $1,399,000 as of this packet's preparation (legacycalifornia.com, Aug 2026), down from an original $1,999,000 ask in late 2025.

Who this suits

Marketing materials pitch three buyer profiles: an investor stabilizing the existing NNN income streams, a hospitality operator building toward a multi-key boutique lodging concept, and a lifestyle buyer who occupies the house and lets the studio income offset carrying costs. None of the three requires rezoning — water and sewer are both public, which removes the biggest ceiling a rural property like this usually carries. The remaining open question is what C‑1 actually permits for overnight lodging and events (see Zoning).
