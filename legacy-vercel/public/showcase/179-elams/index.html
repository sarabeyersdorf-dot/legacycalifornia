Tucked into the Sierra foothills just minutes above Lake Oroville, this private 30-acre retreat pairs a cozy home with serious shop space and room to roam. The single-level, 2004-built residence offers one bedroom and one bath, plus a retreat and recreation room that flexes easily as a den, home office, or second bedroom.

Attached to the home is the real showstopper: a 1,400+ sq. ft., three-bay garage and shop with its own central heat and air — plenty of room for vehicles, a boat, an RV, or a full workshop, with space left over for hobbies or storage.

Whether you're after a weekend basecamp, a full-time foothill retreat, or a blank canvas with a workshop already built in, 179 Elams Ranch Rd delivers space, privacy and recreation just minutes from the water.

04 — The shop

Three bays.
Heated and
cooled.

Most rural properties at this price make you choose between a house and a place to work. This one doesn't. The attached 1,400+ sq. ft. shop is nearly twice the footprint of the residence, runs on its own central heat and air, and opens through three garage-door bays onto a circular gravel drive.

Park the boat and the truck inside and still have room for a bench, a lift, or a season's worth of gear. There's 220V power at the property, laundry hookups in the garage, and a covered carport space besides.
